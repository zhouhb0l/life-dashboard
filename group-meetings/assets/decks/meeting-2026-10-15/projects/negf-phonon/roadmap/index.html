Cubic phonon scattering in NEGF

Mass-weighted displacements + a symmetric cubic vertex.

H₃ = 1/3! ∑abc Ψabcuaubuc\[H_3=\frac{1}{3!}\sum_{abc}\Psi_{abc}u_a u_b u_c\]
Gr(ω) = [(ω+i0+)²I − D − ΣLr − ΣRr − Σppr]−1\[G^r(\omega)=\left[(\omega+i0^+)^2I-D-\Sigma_L^r-\Sigma_R^r-\Sigma_{pp}^r\right]^{-1}\]
Σpp,ab<,>(ω) = iℏ/2 ∑ ΨacdΨbef ∫ dω′/(2π) Gce<,>(ω′)Gdf<,>(ω−ω′)\[\Sigma_{pp,ab}^{<,>}(\omega)=\frac{i\hbar}{2}\sum_{cdef}\Psi_{acd}\Psi_{bef}\int\frac{d\omega'}{2\pi}G_{ce}^{<,>} (\omega')G_{df}^{<,>} (\omega-\omega')\]

Convention: causal retarded reconstruction; no fitted prefactor.
