What the cubic scattering bubble means

A vibration couples to a pair of other modes. This interaction shifts its resonance, broadens its response and exchanges energy.

Green function: propagation

\(G^r\): response with amplitude and phase.
\(G^{<,>}\): fluctuations and occupations.

\[G^r=\big[(G_0^r)^{-1}-\Sigma_{pp}^r\big]^{-1}\]
\[(G_0^r)^{-1}=(\omega+i0^+)^2I-D-\Sigma_L^r-\Sigma_R^r\]

\(G_0\): harmonic device with contacts. \(D\): mass-weighted dynamical matrix.

Self-energy: interaction feedback

\(\operatorname{Re}\Sigma_{pp}^r\): resonance shift.
\(\operatorname{Im}\Sigma_{pp}^r\): damping and broadening.

\[G^{</>}=G^r\big(\Sigma_L^{</>}+\Sigma_R^{</>}+\Sigma_{pp}^{</>}\big)G^a\]

Contacts and phonon interactions jointly set the fluctuations.
\(G^a=(G^r)^\dagger\).

\[\Sigma_{pp,ab}^{</>}(\omega)=\frac{i\hbar}{2}\sum_{cdef}\Psi_{acd}\Psi_{bef}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{d\omega'}{2\pi}\,G_{ce}^{</>}(\omega')\,G_{df}^{</>}(\omega-\omega')\]

Two vertices \(\Psi\Psi\) × two internal lines \(GG\), summed over every allowed pair. \(\Psi\): cubic force constants in mass-weighted coordinates.

Born: both internal lines use fixed harmonic \(G_0\). Evaluate scattering once.

SCBA: both use dressed \(G\). Changed spectra and occupations feed back into the same bubble.

The same bubble contains decay and combination channels. SCBA retains this selected diagram topology.

User-requested physical interpretation ties the self-energy bubble to its channels instead of presenting disconnected decay/coalescence cartoons. G^r is a causal vibrational response, with amplitude and phase, not a transmission probability; Gless/greater additionally encode statistical correlations and occupations. Sigma_pp is an interaction correction to propagation. In the passive positive-frequency regime its real retarded part shifts resonances and its imaginary part produces damping/broadening. The two cubic vertices and two internal propagators represent coupling to a pair of vibrational fluctuations; the frequency convolution and internal index/q sums include all allowed pairs. The dashed cut is an interpretive link to physical energy-exchange channels, not a spatial separation or a claim that the two lines are always positive-frequency daughters. Decay uses externalomega=omega1+omega2; combination keeps the same observed externalomega incoming, omega+omega1=omega2, arising from signed-frequency/occupation terms of the same bubble. Sketches are quasiparticle interpretations, not literal trajectories. Born substitutes harmonic/contact-dressedG0 on both internal lines; SCBA uses dressedG and iterates the spectral/statistical feedback. It does not include every higher-order vertex correction. The current Guo bubble branch excludes static tadpole and quartic interactions. Visible formulas restored at the user’s request: Dyson with contact-dressed G0 and its full inverse definition, Keldysh relation, and exact real-space lesser/greater cubic bubble with i hbar/2 and full signed-frequency integral. The two cubic vertices and two internal lines in the diagram map directly to the two Psi factors and two Green functions in this equation. The mass-weighted definition is retained below. Retained formal expressions from the earlier firstslide: \(H=H_2+H_3,\ H_3=\frac1{3!}\sum_{abc}\Psi_{abc}u_au_bu_c,\ u_a=\sqrt{M_a}\Delta r_a,\ \Psi_{abc}=\Phi^{(3)}_{abc}/\sqrt{M_aM_bM_c}\). Three-phonon scattering and energy conservation

Cubic interactions split and combine phonons, redistributing energy between frequencies and transverse momenta.

\[\Sigma_{pp,ab}^{</>}(\omega)=\frac{i\hbar}{2}\sum_{cdef}\Psi_{acd}\Psi_{bef}\int\frac{d\omega'}{2\pi}G_{ce}^{</>}(\omega')G_{df}^{</>}(\omega-\omega')\]
\[q=q'+q''+\mathbf G,\qquad \Sigma_{pp,q}\ \propto\ \frac{1}{N_q}\sum_{q'}\left|\Psi(q,q',q-q')\right|^2\,G_{q'}G_{q-q'}\]

Heat current into contact-connected device

\[\begin{aligned}J_\alpha&=\operatorname{Re}\int_0^\infty\frac{d\omega}{2\pi}\hbar\omega\\&\quad\times\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\Sigma_\alpha^>G^<-\Sigma_\alpha^<G^>\right]\end{aligned}\]
\[J_L+J_R=0,\qquad P_{pp}=\int C_{pp}(\omega)\,d\omega=0\]

Physical checks

  • Equal reservoir temperatures give zero net heat current.
  • Energy is conserved; phonon number need not be conserved.
  • Spectral redistribution can be nonzero even when its energy integral vanishes.

Conservation concerns total energy exchange, not zero scattering at each frequency.

Both Jα are positive into the device. Heat-flux density requires a separate area normalization.

Sources: Guo2021 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.103.174306 (author manuscript https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.10087), Eqs1–9; Guo2020 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.102.195412. The first equation is the real-space cubic bubble; the second is a scalar schematic transverse sum with the two cubic vertices represented by |Psi|^2, while tensor contractions and frequency convolution are suppressed. Bosonic contact convention: Sigma_alpha^<=-i nB Gamma_alpha, Sigma_alpha^>=-i(1+nB)Gamma_alpha. For TL>TR, JL>0 and JR<0 under the into-device convention. Literature right-going flux may use the opposite orientation at R. Cpp denotes spectral collision-energy exchange. Raw harmonic bond current need not be constant for a nonlocal interaction self-energy; the conserved quantity includes interaction exchange. Neither conservation nor SCBA implies phonon-number conservation.
Guo et al., PRB 103, 174306 (2021), Eqs. 1–9

04 · Scattering length and transport regime

Scattering length and the ballistic–diffusive crossover

Compare the anharmonic center length \(L\) with the phonon–phonon scattering length \(\ell\).

\[\kappa_{\mathrm{eff}}(L)=\frac{J}{\Delta T}\frac{L}{A}=\frac{G L}{A}\ \propto\ L^\alpha\]

Fixed cross-section \(A\), \(G=J/\Delta T\), small reservoir \(\Delta T\).

Ballistic: \(L\ll\ell\)
\(G\sim L^0,\quad\kappa_{\mathrm{eff}}\propto L,\quad\alpha=1\)

Diffusive: \(L\gg\ell\)
\(G\propto L^{-1},\quad\kappa_{\mathrm{eff}}\sim L^0,\quad\alpha=0\)

Increasing \(L/\ell\) turns ballistic length scaling into a diffusive conductivity plateau.

Conceptual schematic. ℓ varies with mode and frequency. Contact resistance can affect the apparent exponent.

Added at the user's request on 28 October. Native editable conceptual SVG, not a calculated silicon length series or an extracted scattering length. L is the length of the interacting central scattering region and ell is a representative transport-direction phonon–phonon mean free path, with ell_lambda=|v_lambda,z| tau_lambda as a quasiparticle interpretation. It is mode/frequency dependent. The two orange marks illustrate cubic interaction/scattering events, not static impurity atoms. Dashed boundaries indicate harmonic/anharmonic model partitions rather than a demonstrated physical material junction. For the fitted complete-center-energy model, every endpoint derivative of each retained center energy must remain present; the shading should be read as the complete interaction support, not a sharp crop at nominal center-energy layers 2–6. The current guide reports132 active coordinates within216 harmonic coordinates and does not determine a unique physical interface. Effective two-terminal conductivity is defined using reservoir DeltaT and fixed area A. In linear response, G=J/DeltaT, ballistic G~L^0 gives kappa_eff~L and alpha1, while ordinary diffusion with a finite bulk conductivity gives G~L^-1, kappa_eff~L^0 and alpha0. These are asymptotic regimes, not universal exponents for every disordered, anomalous, localized or low-dimensional system. The smooth curve is a qualitative crossover schematic, not fitted data; contact resistance and a broad mean-free-path distribution affect an intermediate apparent exponent. BTE extensions and the unified NEGF picture

BTE approaches use different extensions as transport conditions change. NEGF includes these effects in one open-device formulation.

Physical featureBTE limitation and required extensionIncluded in the same NEGF framework
Large temperature biasSmall-bias linearization misses large deviations.
Nonlinear BTE		Hot and cold reservoirs drive occupations and spectra at the applied bias.
Ballistic → diffusive crossoverBulk conductivity misses finite-length flow.
Finite-device BTE		Ballistic harmonic transport and scattering-limited flow follow the same equations.
Temperature-dependent spectraA fixed spectrum misses resonance changes.
Renormalized / self-consistent BTE		Occupations, resonance shifts and broadening evolve together with scattering.
Phonon statisticsClassical populations miss Bose occupations.
Quantum / Bose BTE*		Bose reservoir occupations and quantum correlations enter directly.
Contacts and interfacesThe bulk model has no explicit contacts.
Boundary / interface BTE		Contacts, geometry and interface coupling enter the device model explicitly.
Wave interferenceDiagonal populations do not retain phase.
Coherence / Wigner extensions		Wave phase, interference and their modification by phonon collisions are retained.
Overlapping resonancesSharp modes miss broad, overlapping spectra.
Generalized quantum kinetics		Broadened spectra and frequency-dependent scattering are treated together.

One NEGF framework treats these effects together without switching transport equations.

* Bose statistics are already standard in phonon BTE. NEGF includes the listed effects within the chosen Hamiltonian and self-energy approximation.

User requested the BTE column to emphasize the limitation of a baseline description and name its required amendment, including nonlinear BTE, finite-device BTE and quantum BTE, while presenting the unified NEGF advantage. Each row now identifies the specific simplifying assumption rather than asserting that every conventional phonon BTE lacks all seven effects. Large-bias limitation belongs to the linearized BTE, finite-device limitation to a bulk conductivity description, missing Bose occupations to the classical limit, spectral-shift limitation to fixed-phonon inputs, and missing wave phase to diagonal populations. Bose-Einstein statistics is already standard in quantum phonon BTE and this correction is visible in the footnote. Finite-domain BTE handles ballistic-diffusive crossover, boundary/interface kernels and contact jumps; nonlinear BTE handles finite bias. Renormalized BTE may use temperature-dependent frequencies and scattering rates; Wigner or density-matrix formulations retain coherences. The final row names generalized quantum kinetics as the extension beyond a sharp-quasiparticle population description, not as one unique established BTE correction or as a claim that Wigner alone solves every off-shell problem. NEGF includes nonequilibrium reservoir occupations, finite geometry, contacts and harmonic wave coherence in one framework; the selected interaction self-energy produces the corresponding spectral shifts, broadening and inelastic exchange self-consistently. This is a formulation advantage, not a claim that every correction is exact or that the current silicon results have demonstrated all proposed regimes. Cubic-bubble SCBA does not automatically add omitted static tadpoles, quartic or higher-order vertices, or validate arbitrary high-temperature, large-bias or strong-anharmonic regimes.
