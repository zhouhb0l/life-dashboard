# Au–MoS₂ — 28 October 2026 meeting

This is the meeting-specific adaptation of the existing five-slide Au–MoS₂
presentation. Open `index.html` through the meeting's Au–MoS₂ project entry.
Earlier meetings and the upstream project are preserved.

The first three slides retain the requested rod-contact order:

1. Four electron-SBH results with extraction-sensitivity whiskers, plus
   atomic cross sections of F10, R10, F15 and R15.
2. F10/R10: spatial PDOS contour, PDOS versus energy relative to the Fermi
   level, and a graphical extraction-sensitivity comparison.
3. F15/R15: the corresponding three evidence figures.

Only electron SBH is displayed. The summary uses the same original
regional far-core estimator for all four cases; contour spatial-edge
guides are supporting evidence, not a different summary estimator. The
81-setting whiskers show extraction sensitivity rather than statistical
confidence or total physical uncertainty. Paired sensitivity figures use
matched F-minus-R differences.

The labels “10” and “15” are retained as case names. They do not mean
literal 10/15 nm rods: actual Au widths are 1.106/0.902 nm for F10/R10
and 1.506/1.317 nm for F15/R15.

remain alongside the separately named presentation figures. See
